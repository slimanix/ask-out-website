# 💖 Cute & Interactive "Ask Out" Website

A fun, charming, and responsive website designed to ask that special someone out on a date!

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dodging "No" Button**: Whenever she tries to hover or tap "No", it teleports away with hilarious phrases, while the "YES" button grows bigger!
- **Cute Animated Mascot**: Classic Milk & Mocha bear animations that react as she hesitates.
- **Victory Celebration**: Explosive confetti shower, sweet sound effects, and happy bear hugs when she clicks "YES".
- **Interactive Date Planner**:
  - Lets her pick date activities (Coffee & Pastries, Romantic Dinner, Sunset Picnic, Movie & Ice Cream, Stargazing, Surprise Me).
  - Food cravings (Italian, Sushi/Ramen, Burgers, Tacos, Desserts).
  - Time & day picker.
- **One-Click WhatsApp / Copy Confirmation**: Automatically generates a formatted text message summarizing her date choices to send directly to your phone.
- **Floating Hearts Background**: Smooth canvas particle animation.
- **Built-in Sound Effects**: Web Audio synthesized chimes and boings (works instantly with no extra audio files).
- **In-Browser Customizer**: Tap the ⚙️ gear icon in the corner to change her name, your name, phone number, and generate a customized shareable link.

## 💌 How to Personalize & Send to Her

### Method 1: Using the Gear Icon (Easiest)
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Click the **⚙️ (Settings)** icon in the top right.
3. Type her name, your name, and your WhatsApp phone number.
4. Click **"Copy Link"** to get your personalized URL (e.g., `?name=Sarah&from=Alex&phone=15551234567`).

### Method 2: Free Instant Online Hosting (To send her a mobile link)
To send it to her phone so it looks like a real website:
- **Option A: GitHub Pages (Free)**
  1. Upload the files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`) to a new GitHub repository.
  2. In repo **Settings** > **Pages**, choose the `main` branch.
  3. Your site is live at `https://yourusername.github.io/repo-name/`!
- **Option B: Netlify Drop / Vercel (Drag-and-Drop in 10 seconds)**
  1. Go to [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
  2. Drag the `ask-out-website` folder right onto the page.
  3. You get an instant live HTTPS link to send her!
